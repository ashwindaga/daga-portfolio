Figures are built from each member's CAMS/RTA consolidated account statement, transaction by transaction, then re-priced using the scheme's own NAV history. XIRR treats every SIP instalment, lumpsum and step-up as its own cash flow on its allotment date. ICICI Prudential Value Fund is held as a closed position: the systematic transfer that fed it ran out of source units in Aug 2024, so no further inflows are assumed unless you tell Claude otherwise. Send an update any time and the figures here will be recomputed from scratch. The index fund comparison on each card puts the same rupees on the same dates into a real index fund (Nifty 500 for flexi cap, multicap, value, focused and contra funds; Nifty Midcap 150 for midcap; Nifty LargeMidcap 250 for large and midcap; Nifty 50 for large cap, for hybrid funds, and for holdings that began before Sep 2019, when the Nifty 500 index fund did not yet exist). Hybrid funds hold debt as well as equity, so an all-equity index is a demanding yardstick for them.
